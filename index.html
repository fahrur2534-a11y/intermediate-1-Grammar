INTERMEDIATE GRAMMAR TASK

Topic: Adverbs of Frequency
Level: Intermediate (B1)
Instructions:
Choose the correct adverb of frequency to complete each sentence.

1. Dayat ______ loses her keys. She loses them twice a week.

2. Dito and Hilmi ______ hang out together after school.

3. Amy ______ recognises the constellations in the sky.

4. It is ______ clear in their town because of light pollution.

5. Dito can ______ answer the yellow questions correctly.

6. The Northern Lights are ______ green.

7. Sopi says people ______ see the Northern Lights in their country.

8. Alfi ______ learns something new from Amy.

9. The weather there is ______ cloudy at night.

10. Amy is very intelligent, so she ______ wins at quiz games.

Answer Key

  1. often
  2. usually
  3. sometimes
  4. hardly ever
  5. never
  6. usually
  7. never
  8. often
  9. often
  10. often
